The scale stopped at 72 kg.

I looked at the number for a while.

Not because it was surprising. I had known for some time that I was gaining weight. But seeing it displayed so clearly felt different.

A number on a screen. A small, undeniable fact.

I remember thinking: maybe I should do something.

I started with small changes.

Less fried food. Less late-night snacking. More walking.

Then came the gym.

At first, I was only trying to lose weight. But slowly, something else changed.

The gym became a place where I could still feel some control.

The world outside was complicated. Work was uncertain. Life was always moving faster than I wanted.

But inside that room, things were simple.

You lift the weight.

You finish the set.

You leave.

No one cared who you were or what you had failed at that day.

Only whether you showed up.

The number on the scale slowly went down.

72 kg.

70 kg.

68 kg.

Eventually, 63 kg.

Those numbers were not just numbers anymore.

They reminded me that my life was still something I could influence.

Not everything.

But something.

Around the same time, I began to understand something about relationships.

I used to think love was mostly about feeling.

If two people cared about each other, things would naturally work out.

But life is not that simple.

Two people can love each other and still misunderstand each other.

Two people can want the same thing and still fail to say it clearly.

I learned that silence does not always mean understanding.

Sometimes, it is just silence.

I learned to speak more honestly.

To say what I wanted.

To say what bothered me.

To stop expecting another person to read my mind.

I don't know if those changes made me a better person.

Maybe they just made me more aware of myself.

These days, I still go to the gym.

I still watch what I eat.

But I no longer think about it as a battle.

It has simply become part of my life.

Like taking the subway.

Like preparing tomorrow's lunch.

Like setting an alarm before going to bed.

Tomorrow at seven, it will ring again.

Line 2 to Line 6.

At the office by 8:45.

Lunch is already in the rice cooker.

That is enough.
